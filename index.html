Turn long gameplay videos into scroll-stopping shorts.

Elisha-videos is my personal video studio. It finds the best moment in a long video, cuts it into a 9:16 short with title text, sound effects and captions, then publishes it to my own TikTok and YouTube accounts. The app runs locally on my computer. This page shows how it works and the clips it produces.

How it works

1

Upload

Drop a long video (gameplay, movie) into a project and choose 9:16 or 16:9.

2

Find the best scene

A local scene finder ranks moments by motion and cuts, then I pick the one that grabs attention.

3

Edit

Cut, crop to vertical, add title text, music, sound effects and captions, all rendered locally with ffmpeg.

4

Publish

Fill in caption, privacy and interaction settings, confirm, and post to my own TikTok / YouTube account.

Clips made with the app

Vertical shorts (1080×1920 originals, compressed for the web).

Publishing to TikTok

Login Kit: I connect my own TikTok account. The app shows my display name and avatar so I can confirm where the post goes.

Content Posting API: before each post the app loads my creator info, then shows the caption, a privacy selector built from the allowed options, and Comment / Duet / Stitch settings. Nothing is posted until I review it and press Post. Optionally a clip can be sent to my TikTok drafts instead.

Data: tokens and videos stay on my computer. No data is sold or shared. See the Privacy Policy.

The app interface (front-end only) is in app.html. It needs the local server on the owner's computer to work, so opened here it only shows the layout.
