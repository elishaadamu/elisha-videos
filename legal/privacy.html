Privacy Policy: Elisha-videos

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Elisha-videos ("the App") is a personal tool that edits the owner's own videos into short clips and publishes them to the owner's TikTok and YouTube accounts. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): your display name and avatar, shown so you can confirm which account you are posting to.
  • Creator info (via the Content Posting API): allowed privacy options, interaction settings and maximum video length, so the post form only offers valid choices.
  • Access tokens: issued by TikTok after you sign in, used only to post the videos you choose.

2. What we do with it

The data is used only to publish videos you choose to your own TikTok account and to show you the result. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

3. Where data is stored

The App runs locally on the owner's computer. Videos, captions and TikTok access tokens are stored only on that computer. The App has no external database. Videos are sent only to TikTok (and YouTube, if you choose) when you publish.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account in the App or revoke access in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Disconnecting deletes the stored token. Local video files can be deleted by the owner at any time.

5. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.
